How to read dates. “Confirmed” means the date comes from the issuer’s/central bank’s/government’s own notice, or from a reference calendar (NCSL, Vote411, Wikipedia) that cites official sources. “Estimate” is a third-party aggregator’s guess; “unverified” is this project’s issuer-cadence guess. The daily crawler swaps both for announced dates and, after the event, attaches the official release.

Yields: US Treasury, ECB, Bank of England, Bank of Japan/MoF, Bank of Canada and RBA official series; none is shown for the SNB, RBI or PBoC because no free official daily feed exists. News ticker: headline links from Bloomberg, Financial Times and The Economist public RSS feeds; articles open on the publishers’ sites. FX: ECB euro foreign exchange daily reference rates (one fixing per working day, not live quotes). Conflict counts: ACLED.
